Chaitanya Dwivedi

Senior Research Scientist, Training Dynamics · Institute of Foundation Models (IFM), Sunnyvale, CA

I am a Senior Research Scientist at the Institute of Foundation Models (IFM), working at the interface of LLM training dynamics and compute-efficient scaling. My research explores how to systematically scale model intelligence across the training lifecycle, spanning pre-training (scaling laws, capability proxy metrics, and sparse architectures), progressive model growth, mid-training data mixtures, and reinforcement learning.

At IFM, I study recurrent-depth language models, tracking how depth recurrence shapes representation and capability emergence, alongside discrete diffusion models and pre-training/distillation recipes for the K2-Horizon open-source model suite (0.9B–375B; 680K+ Hugging Face downloads). Previously at Amazon, I led ~trillion-parameter scale ultra-sparse MoE architecture design and pre-training for Amazon Rufus (300M+ customers, $10B+ annualized sales), delivering base models that matched or surpassed the open-source SOTA at the time (DeepSeek-V3-Base and Kimi-K2-Base). Earlier, I led multimodal graph-fusion pathology research at PathAI and completed my Master’s at Carnegie Mellon University on Bayesian deep learning, advised by Dr. Gauri Joshi.

I am also an avid open-source contributor to widely used LLM evaluation and distributed-training frameworks, including Terminal-Bench, Harbor adapters, NVIDIA Megatron-LM, and NVIDIA NeMo.

Research Themes

Scaling Laws & Capability Proxies

IFM · LoRM · K2-Horizon

Co-authored controlled scaling laws for latent-feedback Transformers (LoRM: up to 48% lower compute and 47% less unique data) and formulated likelihood-, entropy-, and rank-based capability proxies predicting downstream reasoning, math, and coding on held-out larger models (Kendall’s τb = 0.946), replacing validation-loss scaling laws in recipe selection, alongside pre-training and distillation recipes for the K2-Horizon fleet (0.9B, 3.7B, and 7B frontier performance in their size classes at release).

Sparse MoE, Model Growth & Recurrent Depth

IFM · Rufus · Expert Upcycling

Applied scaling laws and model-hardware codesign to design the sparsity and attention architecture for Amazon Rufus’s ~trillion-parameter scale ultra-sparse MoE under quality and serving constraints, resolving multi-head latent attention (MLA) and virtual pipeline parallelism bottlenecks to achieve 2× cluster MFU, 50% lower serving latency, and a +22% quality gain over the prior production baseline, while reactivating 75% of dormant experts (up to +10% downstream quality). Developed gradient-guided Expert Upcycling (-32% GPU-hours) and study recurrent-depth MoE growth alongside KV-cache sharing for memory-efficient long-context inference.

Mid-Training, Agentic RL & Fast Decoding

IFM · Amazon · Uno · Translate-R1

Curated mid-training data mixtures (up to +10% on AIME’25 and SWE-bench) and persona-driven DPO recipes (+8 percentage points over SFT across 15 benchmarks) for Rufus, alongside asynchronous VERL GRPO (2× RL throughput). Co-developed Uno lossless discrete-diffusion decoding (2.2× faster generation, up to 40% faster RL rollouts) and Translate-R1 cost-aware GSPO RL (EACL 2027 Oral), while studying trajectory length-penalty optimization, reasoning judges, and Terminal-Bench.

Personalized Voice & Conversational Retrieval

Amazon Alexa · EMNLP 2023

Led efforts to design, train, and deploy three query-correction and global-index personalized dense voice retrieval models across U.S. production traffic on 500M+ Amazon Alexa devices, achieving +80% recall and +91% entity retrieval (EMNLP 2023 Best Industry Paper Award).

Multimodal Clinical Graph Pathology

PathAI · AIM-Fusion · Gilead

Developed non-registered multi-stain CNN–GNN pathology models (+20% weighted Cohen’s kappa across 5,160 liver biopsy pairs; CVPR 2022 Live Oral, cited in Nature Medicine and Medical Image Analysis) and productized them as AIM-Fusion across 1,383 paired NASH clinical-trial biopsies with Gilead Sciences, validated against FibroScan, morphometry, and RNA-seq (AASLD 2022 Oral, Hepatology).
